# 15 — Narrative Beats & First 10 Hours

## Narrative Design Rules
1. **Sandbox first** — story never locks the loop for long.  
2. Lore is **biased** (Council docs lie; cults rant; beasts dream true-ish).  
3. Player origin is a **slow peel**, not a trailer spoiler dump.  
4. Notoriety is the real main quest spine.

---

## Opening (0:00–0:25) — “Discarded”

**Cold open (optional short):** blurred academy corridor, gold sigils, a voice: *“Failure is a kind of mercy.”* Hands shove. Fall through light.

**Wake:** Thornwake Glade, rain of seal-ash, weak wand, empty satchel, split lip.  
**No name confirmation.** Journal starts with `???` and three scratched lines.

**Scripted-but-skippable goals:**
1. Stand, gather, craft binding chalk  
2. Encounter Glimmerpouch (comedy tutorial capture)  
3. Find Discarding Stones (worldbuilding)  
4. Night 1: Lantern Hare or die-adjacent scare  

**Feeling:** small, angry, curious — not destined.

## Memory Layers (origin)

Unlocked by **Memory Shards** (ruins, dreams, beast echoes). Player can ignore forever.

| Layer | Possible reveal | Gameplay unlock |
|-------|-----------------|-----------------|
| L1 | You were in a named cohort | Journal names |
| L2 | Experiment or bloodline test | +1 capture method |
| L3 | Who ordered the discard | Personal nemesis among Council |
| L4 | What the ritual almost did | Unique endgame ritual option |

**Nobody path:** if player skips shards, L4 can still frame them as “proof destiny was always a lie” — valid and strong.

---

## Path Fork Scene (design)

At a broken amphitheater:
- **Vita:** restore the living circle; beasts nearby calm; permanent Bloom seed at base  
- **Mortis:** ignite the brandpit; thrall a watcher-beast; permanent Heat + production spike  

Can hybrid later but this choice sets **UI chrome, music stem, and first exclusive recipe**.

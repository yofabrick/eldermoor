# 24 — Enemies & Non-Beast Threats

Beasts are catchable economy units. **Threats** are pressure, story, and combat variety. Not everything should be a pet.

---

## Threat Classes

| Class | Examples | Design job |
|-------|----------|------------|
| **Wild hazards** | Mana storm, poison fog, Seal Echo | Preparation & routing |
| **Vermin** | Ash ticks, seal-moths | Early combat trash |
| **Predators (non-catch)** | Night crawlers | Night fear |
| **Corrupted** | Veil-scarred husks | Mid combat |
| **Humanoids** | Scavengers, cultists | Moral & loot |
| **Institutional** | Watchers, Inquisitors, strike teams | Heat fantasy |
| **Siege** | Expedition constructs | Base defense |

## Inquisitor Combat Fantasy
They should feel **trained**, not tankier trash:
- Ward discipline (your Bind Thread weaker on them)  
- Beast-slayer tools (silence your party pets briefly)  
- Call for retreat at 20% — optional chase for loot vs Heat spike  

Killing Envoys early is *possible* and *loud*.

---

## Base Raid Night (slice)
Trigger: Heat ≥4 or scheduled every N nights after tower built.  
Waves: vermin → husks → 1 humanoid elite.  
Win: survive until dawn bell.  
Fail: damaged modules, stolen storage portion, beasts injured (not permadeath default).

## Environmental Hazards

| Hazard | Counter |
|--------|---------|
| Poison fog (Mire) | Mire Filter / high ground / potions |
| Mana storm | Wait, or Mortis thrall batteries / Vita anchors |
| Seal Echo (anti-magic) | Beast combat, mundane tools, leave zone |
| Night cold strain | Fire, coats, Hearthkin aura |

---

## Design Rule
If a creature would be more fun **owned**, make it a beast.  
If it exists to **pressure power fantasy**, keep it a threat.

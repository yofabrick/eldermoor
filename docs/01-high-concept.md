# 01 — High Concept

## Working Title
**Eldermoor**  
*Setting / region name:* **The Wild Arcana**

## Elevator Pitch (15s)
You wake up with broken memories in a sealed-off magical wilderness. Catch original beasts, force them to work or fight, build your own mage enclave, choose Life magic or Death magic, and grow powerful enough that the High Council itself comes for you.

## Longer Pitch (60s)
The great academies and High Council sealed away the Wild Arcana after the Veil War. Decades later the seals crack. You are a cast-out, failed student, or experiment — you don’t fully know which. With a weak wand and raw will, you survive by binding magical beasts as workers, mounts, weapons, and research subjects. You expand a base from tent to private academy, automate production, and shape the world through Vita (Life) or Mortis (Death). When your power can no longer be ignored, the institutions that discarded you declare war — and you decide whether to destroy, reform, or replace them.

## Fantasy Promise
> “What if I could *live* in a dangerous open magic world — keep my own beasts, build my own power base, use dark magic if I want, and do it with friends — without being stuck as a schoolkid on rails?”

## Target Audience
| Segment | Why they care |
|---------|----------------|
| Hogwarts Legacy fans who finished the story and want *more agency* | Sandbox + multiplayer + beasts as systems |
| Palworld / Ark / Survival players who want wizard fantasy | Familiar loop, new skin and magic depth |
| Adult millennials with Wizarding nostalgia who aged past “pure family” | Satire, moral grey, freedom |
| Co-op / friends groups | Shared bases, raids, territory, duels |

**Primary age:** 16–35  
**Tone rating target:** Teen / Mature-leaning (violence against creatures optional, dark magic, exploitation satire — not pure kids)

## Unique Selling Points
1. **Creature economy as the heart** — beasts are labor, combat, transport, and story, not side pets.  
2. **Vita vs Mortis** as a permanent identity choice that rewrites systems, not a cosmetic alignment bar.  
3. **Anti–chosen-one fantasy** — you start as discarded trash; power is taken, not granted.  
4. **Institutional endgame** — the High Council and academies become the late-game faction war, not a single scripted boss.  
5. **Palworld loop + wizard fantasy + free magic** in one package no rights-holder will ship.  
6. **Multi-method capture with opportunity windows** — skill expression, not “HP low → ball.”  
7. **Council Heat** — power generates political consequence; the world hunts success.

## Trailer Promise (first 30 seconds must show)
1. Discarded wake-up in wild magic (not a school tour)  
2. Capture that looks *played*, not menu’d  
3. Beast chopping wood at a growing tower  
4. Vita bloom vs Mortis brand split flash  
5. Grey-gold Inquisitor on the ridge watching *you*

## Demo Law (v0.3 — non-negotiable)
Any playable demo by **minute 15** must include:
1. **Bond Ring** capture with a readable Soften key  
2. **Beast producing** at a station while the player is away  
3. **Heat foreshadow** (pamphlet and/or Watcher)

## Differentiators vs “Palworld + wands” (v0.3)
- Multi-method capture with per-species Soften keys  
- **Seal Echo** zones (player magic suppressed → beasts carry combat)  
- **Council Heat** as early political loop, not only endgame cutscene  
- Vita/Mortis as economy + ending identity, not cosmetic alignment  
- Institutional war fantasy (Academies / High Council)

## Non-Goals (explicit)
- Not a school sim with houses and class schedules as the main loop  
- Not a pure story RPG with linear chapters  
- Not a live-service gacha monetization trap  
- Not a 1:1 IP clone (legal and creative failure mode)

## Success Metrics (concept stage)
- Vertical slice: “I want one more expedition” within 30 minutes  
- Capture → assign work → return with better gear feels complete in <10 min cycles  
- Players can explain Life vs Death difference after 2 hours without a tutorial dump  
- Multiplayer session of 4 feels like *shared ambition*, not lobby chaos  

## Platforms (intent)
PC first (Steam Early Access model like Palworld). Consoles after core loop is proven.

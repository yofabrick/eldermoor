# 02 — Story Bible

## World Premise

The great magical institutions — **Academies** and the **High Council** — won the **Veil War** at catastrophic cost. To retain control, they sealed vast regions of raw, ungovernable magic behind permanent wards: the **Wild Arcana**.

Decades later, the seals fracture. Ancient forests, floating ruins, black mires, sky-isles, and buried vaults reopen. Beasts that should have stayed extinct stir. Corrupted mages and cults return. Magic itself grows wilder and less obedient to school formulas.

The institutions are weaker than they admit. They still claim absolute authority — and still fear what the Wild Arcana can birth.

---

## You: Who You Are at Start

### What you *know*
- Cold stone floors. Academy corridors. Voices of Councilors.  
- A forbidden ritual or experiment. Panic. Then nothing.  
- Someone called you a **failed, disposable student** — or you believe that of yourself.  
- You have a weak wand, basic theory, and the will to not die.

### What you *don’t* know
Your true origin is **layered discovery**, not a cutscene dump. The game never forces a single truth on day one.

### Possible truths (player-found, branchable)
| Thread | Revelation |
|--------|------------|
| **Vessel** | You are the product of a secret academy experiment: a living vessel meant to bind the wild Ur-Magic of the Arcana — now unbound. |
| **Bloodline** | You carry the last remnant of a line that *ended* the Veil War. The Council fears that name. |
| **Nobody** | You really were nobody. Chance, stubbornness, and stolen power made you a threat larger than prophecy. |

**Design rule:** You begin as **outcast / nobody**. You become legend only through action. No “you are the chosen one” tutorial title card.

---

## Narrative Structure

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Sandbox spine** | Survive, expand, specialize, dominate territory |
| **Optional lore** | Ruins, beast memories, documents, dreams, NPC envoys |
| **Faction pressure** | Council attention scales with your power / notoriety |
| **Endgame war** | Institutions can no longer ignore you |

Story is **light on rails, heavy on consequence**. The main plot is the player’s rise; the world reacts.

---

## Factions

### High Council
Aristocratic, bureaucratic, terrified of uncontrolled magic. Speaks in legitimacy and “order.” Deploys Inquisitors first, armies later. Face of pressure: **Inquisitor Mael** (polite, lethal; calls you “Unlisted”).

### Remaining Academies
Teaching centers that double as fortresses. Some faculty want reform; most want you contained or dissected. World space: **Hollow Academies** as dungeon-campus hybrids.

### Free Enclaves / Wild Mages
Other survivors and exiles in the Arcana. Trade, ally, or raid. Multiplayer-friendly faction shell. Early contact: **Calder Renn** (fence/trader).

### Path Mentors (soft)
- **Sister Vale** — Vita remnant; deserted purge missions  
- **Ash-Scribe Orun** — Mortis voice; seals as crime against power  

### Cults of the Veil
Want seals fully shattered or the old war reignited. Tempt Mortis players; horrify Vita purists.

### Beast Courts (optional mid-late)
Rare, near-sapient apex beings that treat human mages as pests, partners, or prey depending on path.

See also: `15-narrative-beats.md` for first 10 hours and NPC table.

## Fundamental Choice: Vita vs Mortis

Unlocks early-mid game. Can mix lightly; **extreme commitment** is heavily rewarded and transforms almost everything.

### Vita — Life Magic
- Growth, binding-as-partnership, healing, creation, reclamation of dead zones  
- Beasts become loyal partners; breeding and long-term power shine  
- Playstyle: sustainability, defense, restorative world control  

### Mortis — Death Magic
- Dominance, corruption, sacrifice, control of death, short-term spikes  
- Beasts can be enslaved, mutated, sacrificed for power  
- Playstyle: aggression, armies of thralls, brutal shortcuts  

**Affects:** beast behavior & skill trees, spell lists, NPC reactions, alliances, endings, even base aesthetics.

See `05-systems-magic.md` for mechanical detail.

## Endgame: War Against the Institutions

When your influence in the Wild Arcana cannot be denied:

1. **Inquisitors & hunters** enter your region.  
2. **Expedition armies** siege or raid player territories.  
3. **The High Council itself** confronts you — in the Arcana or in their remaining fortresses and academies.

### Ending vectors (examples)
| Path | Outcome fantasy |
|------|-----------------|
| **Annihilation** | Burn the institutions; force a new era of free growth (Vita) or death-rule (Mortis). |
| **Usurpation** | Topple the Council and sit the throne yourself. |
| **Reform** | Hardest: conditions, alliances, and restraint required. |
| **Unsealing** | Shatter all remaining wards; magic free and wild for everyone — chaos ending. |

Endgame is not “one boss, credits.” It is the **capstone of base + beasts + magic path + multiplayer alliances**.

## Themes
- Power taken vs power granted  
- Institutions that protect by smothering  
- Cute / wondrous magic used for labor and war (satire)  
- Freedom has a body count  
- Identity built from fragments, not destiny

## Tone in Story Delivery
Wonder first, then teeth. Lore is optional, atmospheric, and often biased (Council documents lie). Humor is dry and slightly cruel — never pure parody that kills awe.

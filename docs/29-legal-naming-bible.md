# 29 — Legal & Naming Bible (Practical)

**Not legal advice.** Use for creative discipline and to brief actual counsel before public marketing / Steam page.

---

## Hard Rules (Creative)
1. **No** names, places, spells, creatures, organizations, or slogans from existing wizarding franchises.  
2. **No** “almost” spellings (e.g. lookalikes of famous character names).  
3. **No** school-house systems, sorting ceremonies, or iconic scar/lightning brand as identity.  
4. Beasts may share *ecological niche* with familiar fantasy — never protected specific design (e.g. do not clone a known trademarked creature look).  
5. Marketing must sell **Eldermoor / Wild Arcana** fantasy, not “unofficial HP survival.”

## Marketing Phrase Ban List
- “Harry Potter meets Palworld” **as official copy** (press may say it; we don’t lead with IP names)  
- “Hogwarts but…”  
- “Pokémon with guns/magic” as official slogan  
- Any official use of third-party titles in store page  

**Allowed framing:**  
“Open-world magic survival.” “Bind beasts. Build an enclave. Defy the Council.”

## Trademark Hygiene Checklist (before announce)
- [ ] Title search Eldermoor / Wild Arcana in major markets  
- [ ] Logo distinctiveness review  
- [ ] Beast name batch search  
- [ ] Steam page counsel skim  
- [ ] Art review for accidental lookalikes  

## Tone Liability
Dark themes (thrall, sacrifice, labor) must remain **fictional dark fantasy** with toggles; avoid real-world hate symbols or political cosplay of real atrocities.

## Credits Stance
If publicly comparing genres: compare to **systems** (“creature workforce,” “survival sandbox”), not “we replace Franchise X.”

# 04 — Systems: Beasts

## Design Goal
Beasts are the **primary progress currency**: labor, combat, mobility, status, and moral mirror. If beasts feel like optional pets, the game fails.

## Capture Fantasy
Not Pokéballs. Methods stack and specialize:

| Method | Fantasy | Skill |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Bonding spells** | Charm / attune after weakening | Timing, spell skill |
| **Magical snares & circles** | Prepare field, lure, seal | Setup, crafting |
| **Bait & offerings** | Food, shiny objects, rare essence | Knowledge, prep |
| **Ritual binding** | Longer cast, high-tier beasts | Risk, group play |
| **Mortis dominance** | Break will, brand thrall | Power, corruption cost |
| **Vita partnership** | Consent-bond after aid/heal | Patience, support kit |

**Rule:** Capture is an active mini-encounter, not a free inventory click.

---

## Roles (Work & War)

Every beast has **Work tags** and **Combat tags**.

### Work examples
- Lumber / harvest  
- Mine / dig  
- Haul / transport  
- Guard / patrol  
- Craft assist (alchemy, smithing, enchanting)  
- Farm / greenhouse  
- Scout / reveal  

### Combat examples
- Frontline tank  
- Ranged caster-beast  
- Flyer / dive  
- Controller (root, fear, silence)  
- Assassin / ambush  
- Support healer (Vita-leaning)  
- Bomb / sacrifice unit (Mortis-leaning)

Players assign beasts to **stations** at base or take a party into the field.

---

## Progression of a Beast
1. **Wild** → captured  
2. **Bond level** increases via work, combat, feeding, rituals  
3. **Equipment** (collars, runes, saddles, brands)  
4. **Specialization** (job tree or combat tree)  
5. **Breeding / mutation** (mid-late)  
6. Optional **apex evolution** or **Mortis corruption form**

### Vita path effects
- Higher loyalty ceiling  
- Shared passive auras  
- Breeding quality bonuses  
- Refusal of suicidal orders unless bond is extreme  

### Mortis path effects
- Forced obedience  
- Sacrifice recipes for huge temporary power  
- Mutation chains that scare neutrals  
- Risk of backlash / rampage if control slips

## Party & Field Limits
- Active field party size limited (e.g. 3–5) for readability  
- Base workforce soft-capped by pens, food, and management UI  
- Quality > infinite swarm until late Mortis army fantasy intentionally opens up  

## Player Fantasy Checklist
- [ ] “This beast chops wood while I’m gone.”  
- [ ] “This beast is my mount and my friend / slave.”  
- [ ] “I bred something broken and beautiful.”  
- [ ] “I sacrificed three thralls to kill a boss.” (Mortis)  
- [ ] “I rewilded a dead zone with a herd.” (Vita)

# 10 — Competitive Positioning

## Category
**Wizard-fantasy survival sandbox with creature labor economy and institutional endgame.**

## The Gap (why fans are grazeable)

| IP / Game | What fans want | What they get | Opening |
|-----------|----------------|---------------|---------|
| Wizarding World / Hogwarts Legacy | Ongoing sandbox, multiplayer, beasts as systems, freer dark magic | Strong SP story tour; limited post-game systems | **Primary target hunger** |
| Pokémon | Modern freedom, multiplayer depth, adult systems | Slow, controlled, family-first formula | Palworld already proved the model |
| Palworld | — | Best loop reference | We differentiate with magic depth + story identity + endgame war |
| Ark / Valheim / Enshrouded | — | Survival craft competitors | We win on beasts + magic fantasy |
| WoW / New World fantasy MMO | — | Heavy live-ops | We win on personal base + capture fantasy |

## Positioning Statement
For players who loved the *feeling* of a magical world but wanted Palworld-level agency, **Eldermoor** is the open Wild Arcana where beasts work, magic is free, and the High Council is the late-game raid boss of civilization itself.

## Differentiation Matrix

| Feature | Hogwarts Legacy | Palworld | Eldermoor |
|---------|-----------------|----------|-----------|
| Open world | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Creature core loop | Light | Yes | Yes |
| Base automation | No | Yes | Yes |
| Free path magic | Limited | Guns/tech | Vita/Mortis modular |
| Multiplayer day-1 | No | Yes | Yes |
| Institutional endgame story | Fixed SP | Light | Systemic war |
| Legal original IP | Licensed | Original | Original |

## Risk Register
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| “HP clone” PR | Strict original naming; marketing on *Wild Arcana* fantasy, not school nostalgia bait-and-switch |
| Palworld clone PR | Magic identity, path system, endgame war, different combat fantasy |
| Scope explosion | EA vertical slice: 2 biomes, 25 beasts, base v1, co-op 4 |
| Tone backlash | Toggles; satire with wonder; clear rating |
| Multiplayer cost | Friends-first architecture; scale worlds later |

## Go-to-Market Angle
“Palworld proved the loop. The wizard fantasy is still unserved.”

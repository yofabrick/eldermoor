# 30 — Difficulty & Accessibility

## Difficulty Presets

| Preset | Survival | Capture | Heat | Raids | Notes |
|--------|----------|---------|------|-------|-------|
| **Story Wilderness** | Low drain | Wider windows | Slow | Off/rare | Fantasy first |
| **Standard** (default) | Balanced | Normal | Normal | On | Design target |
| **Hard Arcana** | High | Tight windows | Fast | Aggressive | For veterans |
| **Custom** | Sliders | Sliders | Sliders | Sliders | Full control |

### Important sliders
- Hunger / mana drain rates  
- Beast upkeep  
- Opportunity window length  
- Enemy damage  
- Heat gain rate  
- Drop on death %  
- Raid frequency  
- Thrall / gore intensity (content, not difficulty)

## Assist Options (not “easy mode shame”)
- Capture aim assist / sticky soft-target  
- Hold-to-channel vs tap timing  
- Flash reduction  
- Combat telegraph scale  
- Auto-assign recommended job on catch  
- Pathfinding beast unstuck button  

## Accessibility
| Need | Support |
|------|---------|
| Color vision | Path icons + shapes, not hue alone |
| Hearing | Full subtitle + directional combat UI pips |
| Motor | Toggle hold interactions; remap all |
| Cognitive | Optional objective pins; simplify workforce UI |
| Photosensitive | Disable full-screen capture flash |
| Language | UI first EN; DE secondary for our team materials |

## Inclusive Fantasy Notes
- Player body presets adult-only  
- No child-coded workers or thralls  
- Hearthkin are adult-coded spirits  
- Difficulty never gates **story comprehension** behind mechanical elite skill alone (Watchers still appear)

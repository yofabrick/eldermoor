# 27 — Playtest Protocol

## Principles
1. Test **fantasy delivery**, not politeness.  
2. Do not explain the design while they play.  
3. Watch hands and HUD, not only survey scores.  
4. One change at a time between rounds.

---

## Recruit Profile
| Mix | Target |
|-----|--------|
| Survival craft fans | 40% |
| Creature collector fans | 30% |
| Fantasy action / Legacy players | 20% |
| Cold audience (no genre habit) | 10% |
| Avoid: friends who will only praise |

N per gate: **8 minimum**, 12 better.

---

## Session Types

### Gate A — P0 Proto (25 min)
**Script:** “Play until you want to stop or timer ends. Think aloud if natural.”  
**Do not say:** capture, Palworld, Harry Potter.  
**Observe:**
- Time to first gather  
- Time to first capture attempt  
- Time to first successful worker  
- Rage moments / confusion  
**Post survey (5 min):**
1. What was the game about in one sentence?  
2. Most fun moment?  
3. Most frustrating?  
4. Want another session? Y/N  
5. Rate capture feel 1–10  
6. Rate base/work feel 1–10  

**Pass:** ≥75% want another session; median capture ≥6; ≥50% unaided mention “creature works for me.”

### Gate B — P1 Methods (40 min)
Add: “Try catching more than one kind of creature.”  
**Pass:** ≥60% use two different methods; can explain why one failed.

### Gate C — Path fork (50 min)
**Pass:** Players describe Vita/Mortis difference in mechanical terms, not only “good/evil.”

### Gate D — Co-op (60 min, pairs or 4)
**Pass:** Bind score feels fair ≥70%; no one reports “I was useless.”

### Gate E — Slice acceptance (90 min)
**Pass:** See `22-prototype-tickets.md` Definition of Slice Done.

---

## Facilitator Don’ts
- No mid-run tips unless hard stuck >3 min  
- No “actually the design is…” debates in room  
- Record with consent; store anonymized  

## Metrics Dashboard (simple sheet)
`date | build | gate | want_more | capture_score | work_score | recall_capture | recall_work | recall_heat | notes`

## Kill / Pivot Triggers
See `19` and `21`. If Gate A fails twice after fixes → revisit capture fundamentals before art spend.

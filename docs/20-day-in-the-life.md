## Solo player — mid-slice evening (target fun)

**17:40 real-time session start**

You log in at your **Thornwake** cottage. Two Brushbacks idle at the lumber post; storage is fat with planks. Hearthkin pings: craft bench idle. You queue boar-hide pads and a barbed snare.

**Inventory:** glowherb stack low. Pin: Blackvein Mire resin run.

You down a ward tonic, whistle **Ridgespire Juvenile**, fly the treeline. Heat pip is a half-open eye (you branded a Bogwhisper yesterday — Mortis dabbler).

In the mire, poison fog forces a detour. **Lantern Hare** scouts a safe tongue of land. You soft-engage a **Mirelurk Stag**, break its antler plate, drop shiny bait? No — wrong species. Switch to heavy snare + bond thread. Opportunity ring blooms. Commit. Catch.

Modal: **Workforce**. Assign: heavy haul for the planned sky-ruin outpost.

On the way home an **Inquisitor pamphlet** flutters onto the path. Calder’s voice in memory: *“Pay the tax or make them send someone expensive.”*

At base, you drop resin into the still, repair the south ward stone, reassign Glimmerpouch to ruin-scavenge. Journal gains a Memory Shard from the sunken desk you looted — a blacked-out student face that might be yours.

You queue one more expedition because the Ashcrown den marker is only a ridge away.

**Quit-point feeling:** clear next goal, base richer than when you logged in, slight dread of the eye on the HUD.

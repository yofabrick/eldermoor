# 25 — Slice Beast Cards (Full 12)

Each card: fantasy, Soften key, best method, fail lesson, work, combat, path note.  
Greybox can use colored capsules; final needs unique silhouette.

---

## B01 — Glimmerpouch
**Tier I · Glade · Neutral**  
**Fantasy:** Pocket-sized shiny thief; comedy onboarding.  
**Soften key:** Offer **Shiny Tin**; do not heavy-hit (flees).  
**Best method:** Bait → quick snare.  
**Fail lesson:** “It wants glitter, not bruises.”  
**Work:** Scavenge ruins, minor lock-nudge, steal enemy consumable chance.  
**Combat:** Distract, evasion, weak scratch.  
**Personality bark:** *“Mine. Probably.”*  
**Path:** Neutral; Mortis brand makes it steal from *you* sometimes (funny risk).

## B02 — Brushback Boar
**Tier I · Glade · Neutral**  
**Fantasy:** First real worker dopamine.  
**Soften key:** HP mid-band + break tusk guard.  
**Best method:** Snare after charge whiff.  
**Fail lesson:** “Don’t stand in the charge line.”  
**Work:** Lumber light, haul.  
**Combat:** Charge, light tank.  
**Bark:** *“Hrrn.”* (satisfied when working)

## B03 — Lantern Hare
**Tier I · Glade · Vita-lean**  
**Fantasy:** Night safety pet; scout.  
**Soften key:** Calm (no aggression 10s) near light.  
**Best method:** Bonding focus / Living Snare.  
**Fail lesson:** Dominance scares it into uncatchable flee.  
**Work:** Scout perch, herb-sense.  
**Combat:** Flash escape, ally vision.  
**Bark:** Soft chime.  
**Path:** Vita bond strong; Mortis thrall version becomes “dead lantern” scout (edgy, less cute).

## B04 — Cinderscale
**Tier I · Glade/ruin · Neutral**  
**Fantasy:** Forge mascot.  
**Soften key:** Douse rage heat (water/ward) then bait Ember Char.  
**Best method:** Bait + snare.  
**Fail lesson:** Hitting during overheat kills or enrages.  
**Work:** Smelt assist, cook.  
**Combat:** DoT spit.  
**Bark:** Clicking coals.

## B05 — Hearthkin
**Tier I · Ruins · Neutral (ethics fork)**  
**Fantasy:** House-spirit labor — wonder + discomfort.  
**Soften key:** Clean shrine / offer tidy help (Vita) **or** Brand at broken shrine (Mortis).  
**Best method:** Situational.  
**Fail lesson:** Pure DPS fails; they phase out.  
**Work:** Craft speed, tidy (storage sort passive).  
**Combat:** Ward pulse support.  
**Bark:** *“The hearth remembers.”*  
**Path:** Defining early moral texture.

## B06 — Mirelurk Stag
**Tier II · Mire · Neutral**  
**Fantasy:** Mid-slice workhorse.  
**Soften key:** Break antler plate + toxin cleanse once.  
**Best method:** Barbed snare + Bind Thread.  
**Work:** Heavy haul, poison herb harvest.  
**Combat:** Antler crush, toxin.  
**Bark:** Wet bellow.

## B07 — Bogwhisper
**Tier II · Mire · Mortis-lean**  
**Fantasy:** Fear crooner; dark reagents.  
**Soften key:** Silence the croon (Ward interrupt).  
**Best method:** Dominance brand easy; Vita needs cleanse quest.  
**Work:** Essence still, corpse-scent (ruins).  
**Combat:** Fear, DoT moan.  
**Bark:** Wet choir.  
**Path:** Mortis poster child.

## B08 — Verdant Sprawler
**Tier II · Deep Glade · Vita-lean**  
**Fantasy:** Living wall / greenhouse soul.  
**Soften key:** Feed growth essence; never fire.  
**Best method:** Living Snare / bond.  
**Work:** Greenhouse, living wall defense.  
**Combat:** Root snare, slow tank.  
**Bark:** Leaf-friction whisper.  
**Path:** Vita base identity.

## B09 — Ridgespire Juvenile
**Tier II · Cliffs · Neutral**  
**Fantasy:** First true mount.  
**Soften key:** Win pride duel (no cheap DoT cheese) — land three clean hits after perfect Ward.  
**Best method:** Bond after duel; snare alone fails.  
**Work:** Mount only (no station).  
**Combat:** Dive rake.  
**Bark:** Skree of respect.  
**Fail lesson:** “It won’t serve the unworthy.”

## B10 — Umbracourse Foal
**Tier II · Night roads · Mortis-lean**  
**Fantasy:** Night travel king.  
**Soften key:** Night-only window; use Lumen carefully (too much = flee).  
**Best method:** Brand or quiet bond in darkness.  
**Work:** Night caravan.  
**Combat:** Fear aura.  
**Bark:** Hooves like cloth.

## B11 — Skyreel Pup
**Tier II · Sky-ruin edge · Neutral**  
**Fantasy:** Logistics cuteness.  
**Soften key:** Feed cargo-weight puzzle (place mats on platform).  
**Best method:** Bait + bond.  
**Work:** Cargo lift between outposts.  
**Combat:** Static discharge.  
**Bark:** Electric purr.

## B12 — Ashcrown Whelp
**Tier III · Elite den · Neutral**  
**Fantasy:** Slice boss-adjacent dragonlet.  
**Soften key:** Break armor plates in order; avoid enrage stacks.  
**Best method:** Ley snare + duo pact preferred; solo possible but long.  
**Work:** Magma Forge assist.  
**Combat:** Breath cone, wing buffet.  
**Bark:** Crown-crackle.  
**Reward:** Magma Forge module unlock on first bind or first kill trophy.

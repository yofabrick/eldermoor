# 05 — Systems: Magic

## Design Goal
Magic is **modular, experimental, and identity-defining** — not a fixed school curriculum of 15 story spells.

## Resource: Mana & Strain
- **Mana:** regenerates (rest, potions, Vita nodes, thrall drain for Mortis).  
- **Strain:** builds from heavy casting; causes misfires, vulnerability, or temporary corruption.  
- Survival pressure: night, cold, mana drought zones in deep Arcana.

---

## Spell Crafting Fantasy
Spells are combinations of:

| Layer | Examples |
|-------|----------|
| **Root** | Force, Flame, Growth, Shadow, Bind, Sever, Mend |
| **Form** | Bolt, Wave, Ward, Sigil, Familiar-channel, Totem |
| **Modifier** | Pierce, Chain, Persist, Blood-cost, Shared-with-beast |

Not full free-form chaos in v1 — curated modularity with room to discover recipes in ruins.

**Wand customization:** core, length, focus crystal, inscribed runes → changes modifiers and strain profile.

## Vita vs Mortis

### Unlock
Early-mid after first major ruin or first “forbidden” mentor encounter. Tutorial presents both as valid; world judges you later.

### Spectrum, not pure binary
- Light mix allowed for utility  
- **Commitment thresholds** unlock exclusive trees, beast forms, base modules, and endings  
- Going deep one way locks some opposite endgame options (communicated clearly)

### Vita (Life)
| Domain | Examples |
|--------|----------|
| Growth | Accelerate crops, beast growth, living walls |
| Mend | Heal self, beasts, allies; cleanse corruption |
| Bind-as-pact | Stronger voluntary bonds; shared senses |
| Reclaim | Purify blighted land; create safe verdant zones |
| Create | Temporary constructs of vine/light; spawn helpers |

**Fantasy:** gardener-king, sanctuary builder, symbiotic army.

### Mortis (Death)
| Domain | Examples |
|--------|----------|
| Dominate | Break wills; brand thralls |
| Sacrifice | Convert beast/NPC HP into huge burst power |
| Corrupt | Mutate beasts; blight land for resources |
| Sever | Anti-heal, execute, soul-cut elites |
| Raise | Short-lived thralls from remains (not full necromancer clone — distinct visual language) |

**Fantasy:** tyrant-experimenter, war machine, shortcut king.

### Mechanical rewards for commitment
- Exclusive capture methods  
- Exclusive base production buildings  
- World reaction (prices, attacks, allies)  
- Different final war toolkits against the Council

## Dark Arts Policy (tone + systems)
- Available relatively early — **not** locked behind a morality lecture  
- Power fantasy first; consequences second (attention, corruption, beast trust, ending gates)  
- Difficulty / server settings can dial “no consequences” for pure sandbox chaos  
- Satire: institutions call everything they fear “dark,” including free research

---

## Combat Feel
- Third-person action with readable telegraphs (not pure turn-based)  
- Beast AI commands: attack, guard, ability, withdraw, sacrifice (Mortis)  
- Dual fantasy: **you are a mage** and **you are a beast-master** — both must feel good alone and together  

## Progression of Player Power
1. Basic attack + 2 utility spells  
2. Capture tools + first element  
3. Path choice opens  
4. Modular recipes + wand crafts  
5. Ritual magic (base or group)  
6. Siege / army-scale magic for endgame war  

## Forbidden Knowledge
Ruins contain **incomplete grimoire pages**. Completing sets = prestige spells. Multiplayer trading of pages is intentional social glue.

## Seal Echo Zones (v0.3 differentiator)

Old ward residue creates **Seal Echo** cells on the map:

| Effect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Player spells | Cost up, damage/heal down, higher misfire |
| Bind Thread | Still works (capture remains possible) but slower |
| Beasts | Full power — often *stronger* relative to you |
| Mundane tools | Unaffected |
| Fantasy | “You are not the academy’s chosen caster here.” |

**Design job:** force beast-mastery identity; prevent pure glass-cannon mage from ignoring the creature pillar.

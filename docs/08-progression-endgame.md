# 08 — Progression & Endgame

## Player Power Axes
Progress is multi-axis so there is no single “level 50 = done”:

1. **Personal magic** (spells, wand, strain mastery)  
2. **Beast roster quality** (tiers, bond, gear, mutations)  
3. **Base tier & automation**  
4. **World access** (biomes, mounts, seal keys)  
5. **Notoriety / reputation** (Council heat, enclave standing)  
6. **Path depth** (Vita or Mortis commitment)  
7. **Knowledge** (grimoire completion, lore truths about self)

## Soft Gates (not hard walls)
- Gear score-ish via materials  
- Environmental hazards (mana storms, pure sunlight vs umbra beasts)  
- Boss beasts as optional keys  
- Story revelations optional but unlock unique rituals  

## Notoriety System
Actions raise **Council Attention**:
- Large bases, apex captures, public Mortis rites, killing Inquisitors, unsealing ruins  

Attention stages:
1. Rumors  
2. Watchers / spies  
3. Inquisitor strike teams  
4. Expedition army  
5. **Council War** (endgame unlock)

Players can manage heat (bribes, low profile, false flag, or lean into terror).

### Early Heat teaching (v0.3 — required)
Do **not** hide institutions until late game.

| Approx time | Event | Purpose |
|-------------|-------|---------|
| ~1.5–2.5h | Pamphlet / construct drop | World has politics |
| ~4–6h | Watcher on ridge (even mid Heat) | Paranoia fantasy |
| On first tower module | Heat pip becomes obvious | Base = threat signal |
| Path fork | Music + chrome shift | Identity stakes |
| Heat 3 | Envoy contact | Agency: pay, duel, flee, kill |

Demo builds must show Heat pip + at least pamphlet or Watcher.

## Endgame Structure: The Institutional War

### Phase A — Pressure
Randomized or scheduled attacks; defend home; counter-spy.

### Phase B — Campaign
Optional offensive ops:
- Sabotage academy supply  
- Free experimental prisoners (mirror of player origin)  
- Steal Council ward-keys  
- Assassinate / convert named Inquisitors  

### Phase C — Climax
Assault on a major academy or Council seat **or** defense of the Arcana against total purge.

### Phase D — Aftermath
World state changes:
- New vendor rules, beast spawns, biome climates  
- Banner of player faction over former institutions  
- New game+ adjacent “era” content rather than hard credits-only ending  

## Ending Conditions (summary)
See Story Bible. Mechanical requirement examples:
- **Annihilation:** destroy Council seats + win final field battle  
- **Usurpation:** hold capital ritual site for X time with thralls/allies  
- **Reform:** alliance thresholds + limited war crimes + key NPC survival  
- **Unsealing:** complete multi-ruin key set + survive global magic storm event  

## Post-Endgame Hook
New wild magic weather, remnant loyalists, beast court politics, multiplayer seasonal wars — sandbox continues.

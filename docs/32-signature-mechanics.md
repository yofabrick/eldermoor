# 32 — Signature Mechanics (the non-negotiable set)

If a feature fight happens, **these five win**. Everything else yields.

---

## 1. Bond Ring Capture
**Fantasy:** You *read* a creature and commit under pressure.  
**Proof:** Soften keys differ per species; wrong approach fails with a lesson.  
**Fail mode:** HP threshold + single button.

## 2. Beast Labor Economy
**Fantasy:** Your power compounds while you explore.  
**Proof:** Assign → leave → return to more resources; mood/upkeep matter.  
**Fail mode:** Pets that only fight.

## 3. Council Heat
**Fantasy:** Success makes civilization notice you.  
**Proof:** Early pamphlet/Watcher; tower raises pip; Envoy choices.  
**Fail mode:** Only endgame cutscene reveal.

## 4. Vita / Mortis Identity
**Fantasy:** How you take power stains the world.  
**Proof:** Exclusive recipes, workforce rules, land tint, ending vectors.  
**Fail mode:** Alignment bar with same loot tables.

## 5. Seal Echo
**Fantasy:** The wild does not honor academy formulas.  
**Proof:** Zones where player magic weakens and beasts carry the fight.  
**Fail mode:** Just another damage-type resist shrine.

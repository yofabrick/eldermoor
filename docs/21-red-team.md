# 21 — Red Team Critique & v0.3 Fixes

Honest attack on the concept, then the design response. If we cannot answer these, the game is a mood board.

---

## Attacks

### A1 — “It’s just Palworld with wands”
**Valid if:** trailer shows only gun→wand swap, base boxes, cute slaves.  
**Kill condition:** players cannot name a system that isn’t a skin.

### A2 — “It’s Harry Potter fanfic with serial numbers filed off”
**Valid if:** school nostalgia is the marketing hook, or beasts read as Niffler/Hippogriff clones.  
**Kill condition:** legal or audience only cares because of adjacency.

### A3 — “Capture skill is fake depth”
**Valid if:** optimal path is always same method; opportunity window is a timing QT with no read.

### A4 — “Workforce is a spreadsheet chore”
**Valid if:** best play is optimize menus, never look at beasts as characters.

### A5 — “Vita/Mortis is a fake choice”
**Valid if:** one path is 15% stronger or endings are palette swaps.

### A6 — “Story promises Council war the slice never teaches”
**Valid if:** Heat never appears before hour 20; players forget the fantasy.

### A7 — “Co-op breaks capture economy”
**Valid if:** four players trivialize WIL or steal binds without rules.

### A8 — “Scope will murder the studio”
**Valid if:** breeding + megaserver + 100 beasts all promised at announce.

---

## v0.3 Design Responses (LOCKED)

| Attack | Response (now canon) |
|--------|----------------------|
| A1 | **Signature systems that must appear by minute 15 of any demo:** (1) Bond Ring capture, (2) beast at station producing while you leave, (3) Heat eye on HUD. No demo without all three. |
| A2 | **Marketing ban list:** no “wizard school,” “chosen one,” house sorting, lightning scars as brand. Lead with *Wild Arcana / discarded / beasts / Council*. Legal pass on every public beast silhouette. |
| A3 | **Each slice beast has a unique Soften key** (see beast cards). Optimal method differs; wrong method fails educationally. |
| A4 | **Beasts have mood/control and one-line personality barks.** Idle animations sell labor. “Name + job + mood” is the workforce unit, not a number row only. |
| A5 | **Mirror scenarios** in balance harness: same biome clear as pure Vita vs pure Mortis within 10% time, different *texture*. Exclusive recipes are lateral, not strict upgrades. |
| A6 | **Heat teaches early:** pamphlet at ~2h, Watcher silhouette at ~4–6h even at low power if player builds a tower. Fantasy is foreshadowed, not dumped at end. |
| A7 | **Bind tag rules:** damage contribution + method contribution score; top contributor gets bind rights; others get mat share. Host can set FFA. |
| A8 | **Announce only slice truths.** Breeding, megaservers, 100+ dex = post-EA language only. |

---

## New Unique Hooks Added in v0.3

1. **Seal Echo zones** — temporary anti-magic / wild-magic cells that force beast reliance (you alone are weak). Differentiates from Palworld gun fantasy.  
2. **Grimoire pages as multiplayer social glue** — incomplete recipes tradeable; not gacha.  
3. **Unlisted identity** — NPCs refuse your name until Heat/reputation thresholds; mechanical social progression.  
4. **Ethical capture fork on Hearthkin-class** — teaches path without tutorial dump.  

---

## What We Explicitly Will Not “Make Better” by Adding
- Romance meters  
- Full school sim schedule  
- Gacha banners  
- Battle pass as core loop  
- 200-beast launch dex  

More systems ≠ better. **Tighter fantasy delivery** = better.

# 19 — Risks, Open Decisions, Kill Criteria

## Highest Product Risks

| Risk | Why fatal | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|------------|
| **Capture feels dumb** | Whole pitch collapses | Invest in method depth early; playtest weekly |
| **“HP clone” lawsuit / PR** | Marketing death | Original names, no school sim core, legal review of art |
| **Palworld clone fatigue** | “Just guns→wands” | Vita/Mortis + institutional war + magic modularity must show in trailer second 10s |
| **Scope cancer** | Never ships | Slice lock religiously; kill breeding until post-EA if needed |
| **MP cost** | Studio death | Friends co-op first; no megaserver promise at announce |
| **Tone whiplash** | Reviews call it confused | Style bible + one director of tone |
| **Path imbalance** | Community declares one true path | Dual economy design + arena harness |
| **Beast AI pathing hell** | Automation ragequit | Limit station complexity in slice; navmesh budget |

## Kill Criteria (be honest)
Stop or pivot if after slice playtests:
1. Players skip capture to pure craft-combat and still have more fun  
2. Co-op feels worse than solo for the main loop  
3. Outsiders only describe it as “Harry Potter Palworld” and nothing unique sticks after trailer  

## Unique Hooks That Must Survive Cuts
If we can only keep three systems beyond “survival craft”:
1. **Multi-method capture with opportunity windows**  
2. **Beast labor economy**  
3. **Vita/Mortis + Council Heat endgame**  

Everything else is negotiable.

# 11 — Vertical Slice & Roadmap

## Slice Name
**“First Sealbreak”** — prove the fantasy in one sitting.

## Must-Feel Moments (acceptance tests)
1. I almost die the first night, then a beast saves or works for me.  
2. I assign a beast to chop wood and it actually produces while I explore.  
3. Capture feels skillful and exciting, not inventory spam.  
4. I understand Vita vs Mortis as a *real* fork after one ritual.  
5. With a friend, we bind something harder than solo allows.  
6. I want a second expedition immediately after returning to base.

## Slice Scope
| In | Out |
|----|-----|
| 2 biomes | Full world |
| 8–12 beasts | Full roster |
| Bond + snare + bait capture | All methods |
| Base modules: shelter, storage, 3 stations, pen, basic wall | Academy mega-build |
| Starter + 1 mid gear tier | Full tech tree |
| Path fork teaser | Full endgame war |
| Co-op 4 | Megaserver politics |
| 1 elite beast | Council siege |

## Milestone Ladder
1. **Greybox loop** — gather, craft stick, build box, assign dummy worker AI  
2. **First beast** — capture + work + combat command  
3. **Pretty pass** — one biome art, 4 beasts finalish  
4. **Path fork** — one Vita ritual vs one Mortis brand  
5. **Co-op soak** — 4 players 2-hour test  
6. **Slice lock** — trailer + Steam page materials from slice only  

## Team Shape (indie intent)
- 1 design lead  
- 1–2 gameplay engineers  
- 1 systems/online engineer  
- 1 environment + 1 character/beast artist (or outsource beasts)  
- 1 tech art / UI  
- Audio contract

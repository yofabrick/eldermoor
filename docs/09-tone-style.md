# 09 — Tone & Style Guide

## North Star
**Wonder with teeth.** Magical and beautiful first; satirical and mature second. Never pure grimdark sludge, never pure nostalgic comfort blanket.

## Reference Feel (not clones)
| Borrow spirit from | Avoid becoming |
|--------------------|----------------|
| Palworld’s playful exploitation satire | Empty meme-only tone |
| Hogwarts Legacy’s sense of place | School corridor tour |
| Ark/Valheim survival stakes | Pure misery sim |
| Studio Ghibli nature awe (selective) | Toothless whimsy |
| Dark crystal / older fairy tales | Horror-only niche |

## Writing Voice
- Dry wit, slightly cruel observational humor  
- Institutions speak in polished euphemism  
- Wild mages speak practical and irreverent  
- UI flavor text can smirk; quest text should not over-joke  

## Visual Direction
- Readable silhouettes for beasts (collectibility)  
- Biomes that feel *enchanted and hostile*  
- Player gear: practical mage-adventurer, not school uniform  
- Vita: living light, sap, soft gold-green  
- Mortis: deep violet-black, brand-glow, ash rose — elegant, not edgelord clutter  
- Base pieces scale from rustic to monumental  

## Audio Direction
- Choir + folk instruments for wonder  
- Distorted school bells / iron drums when Council arrives  
- Beast VO: expressive without cartoon scream spam  
- Capture success stinger = dopamine hook  

## Content Boundaries
- Beast death / food / sacrifice: allowed, toggleable intensity  
- No sexualization of child-coded characters (none should be child-coded in playable cast)  
- Slavery / thrall fantasy framed as dark power with satire, not endorsement porn  
- Blood and creature harm: stylized enough for broad PC audience, brutal enough for adult fantasy  

## Marketing Line Options
- “The wilderness does not grade you.”  
- “Build a tower. Bind a dragon. Answer the Council.”  
- “They called you a failed student. Become their extinction event.”  
- “Catch. Craft. Conquer. Choose Life — or Death.”

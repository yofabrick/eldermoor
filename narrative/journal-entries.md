## Shard A — Cohort (L1)

Found under a mossed desk in the Dormer Ruin:

> Cohort Nine. Vessel track. Names blacked.  
> Disposition: *Discard if unstable.*  
> Signed with a seal I almost remember pressing my forehead to.

If this is me, I was inventory.  
If this is not me, someone wanted me to think I was.

## Shard B — Blood (L2)

Beast-dream from a Ridgespire nest: a war that ends because a line of mages pour themselves into a ward until nothing is left but a surname the Council forbids speaking.

My hands shake when I write it. I do not write it.

> “Kid, half the Arcana carries forged tragedy. Maybe you’re a vessel. Maybe you’re a clerk who stole the wrong coat. Build the tower anyway.”

I hate that this comforts me.

## After path fork — Vita

The amphitheater breathed. Vines took the cracks like apologies that meant to stay.

I did not become gentle. I became *responsible* for green things that can still die.

## After path fork — Mortis

The brand smelled like wet iron and old exams.

Power arrived on time. Loyalty became a setting. I told myself settings can be changed later.

Later is a country the Council also claims.

# Inquisitor Mael — Voice & Design Sheet

## Role
Human face of Council Heat before full war. Polite extinction event.

## Look
Grey-gold immaculate coat, clean geometry (contrast player’s stripped ruined coat), half-mask optional, thin authority wand. Never dirty unless player forces a real fight.

## Personality
- Precise, unhurried, almost kind  
- Treats player as a **filing error** that became a **policy problem**  
- Respects competence; despises mess  
- Does not monologue origin spoilers; may *hint*

## Do
- Offer structured choices (tax / leave / duel)  
- Remember prior player insults across encounters  
- Comment on Vita bloom vs Mortis blight of the land  

## Don’t
- Cartoon evil laughter  
- Explain entire Veil War  
- Fall in love with the player  
- Be the final boss of the whole game (he is a rung)

## Sample encounter openers
1. “Unlisted. How efficient of them. Inefficient of you to remain visible.”  
2. “Your tower is charming. Charm is not a permit.”  
3. “I have a form for surrender. The ink is still wet. So is the alternative.”  

## Combat notes
Ward-disciplined; briefly silences beast commands; retreats at ~20% first meeting if not cornered — teaches that institutions survive encounters.

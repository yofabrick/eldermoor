# Steam Page Draft — Eldermoor

## Short Description (300 chars max aim)
Bind magical beasts. Build your enclave in the Wild Arcana. Choose Life or Death magic. Rise from discarded nobody to the threat that makes the High Council march to war. Open-world survival sandbox with co-op.

## About This Game
The seals are failing.

After the Veil War, the Academies and High Council locked away the Wild Arcana — a continent of raw magic, ruined lecture halls, and beasts that were never meant to be catalogued again. You were supposed to stay discarded: a failed student, an unlisted experiment, a nobody.

You wake with a weak wand, broken memories, and a choice that will stain the world.

**Catch. Craft. Command. Commit.**

### Beast Economy
Capture original magical creatures with snares, baits, bonding rites, or dominance brands. Put them to work logging, smelting, scouting, and defending — or take them into the field as your war party. Cute is not harmless. Useful is not kind.

### Build an Enclave
Raise a camp into a tower, then a private academy of your own design. Automate production chains. Fortify against night things… and against people who still believe in permission slips for power.

### Vita or Mortis
Life magic grows, heals, and binds through pact. Death magic dominates, sacrifices, and conquers through brand. Hybrid play exists; commitment rewrites your beasts, your base, and your ending.

### Multiplayer From the Start
Share a wilderness with friends. Co-bind elites. Specialize — one of you industry, one of you capture, one of you war. Configurable conflict for community worlds.

### The Institutions Will Notice
Power has a heat signature. Watchers become Inquisitors. Inquisitors become armies. At the end of the climb, the High Council is not a cutscene — it is a war you planned for with every pen, potion, and beast.

## Key Features
- Open-world survival crafting in an original magical wilderness  
- Deep multi-method creature capture  
- Beast workforce + combat party systems  
- Base-building from shelter to fortress-academy  
- Life vs Death magic identity paths  
- Co-op multiplayer focus  
- Soft story, hard agency — optional origin revelations  
- Institutional endgame against Academies & High Council

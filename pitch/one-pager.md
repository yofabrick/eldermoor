# ELDERMOOR — One-Pager v0.2

## Logline
A discarded mage with broken memories builds power in the unsealed Wild Arcana by binding magical beasts, choosing Life or Death magic, and growing until the High Council declares war.

## Genre
Open-world survival · creature collecting · base-building · sandbox multiplayer · action magic

## Problem
Adult wizard-fantasy fans got one strong guided tour, then a vacuum: no creature economy, no private academy-building, weak multiplayer fantasy, fenced dark magic, endless student-on-rails framing.

## Solution
**Eldermoor** runs the proven catch → work → build → fight loop inside an **original** magical wilderness, with multi-method capture skill, Vita/Mortis identity, and an institutional endgame.

## Pillars
1. Beasts work, fight, and define the economy  
2. Base grows camp → private academy  
3. Vita / Mortis rewrites systems  
4. Multiplayer from day one  
5. Nobody → civilizational threat (Council Heat)

## Loop
Base manage → prepare → expedition → capture/combat → process → progression

## Story
Seals from the Veil War fail. You may be a failed student, bloodline remnant, or escaped vessel — discovery optional. Endgame: destroy, usurp, reform, or unseal.

## Slice Proof (“First Sealbreak”)
2 biomes · 12 beasts · capture windows · base automation · path fork · 4p co-op · Ashcrown elite  
Success metric: *“I want one more expedition.”*

## Business
PC Steam Early Access · premium (no paid beasts) · consoles after loop proven

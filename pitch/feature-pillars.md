# Feature Pillars — Marketing & Design Filter

Every feature must serve at least one pillar. If it serves none, cut it.

---

## Pillar 1 — Beasts Are the Game
**Promise:** Every strong creature is labor, weapon, mount, status, or moral choice.  
**Proof features:** multi-method capture, work stations, beast gear, breeding/mutation, sacrifice (Mortis), partnership auras (Vita).  
**Fail state:** beasts are cosmetic pets or pure combat summons with no base utility.

## Pillar 2 — Your Enclave, Your Rules
**Promise:** From ruined glade to private academy/fortress.  
**Proof features:** modular building, automation chains, defenses, path-themed aesthetics, outposts.  
**Fail state:** housing decorator only, or single prebuilt hub.

## Pillar 3 — Magic Without a Hall Monitor
**Promise:** Modular spells, early dark access, Vita/Mortis identity.  
**Proof features:** spell recipes, wand craft, corruption/strain, exclusive path trees.  
**Fail state:** 12 fixed story spells; dark magic locked to DLC or ending slideshow.

## Pillar 4 — Multiplayer Is Native
**Promise:** Shared wilderness ambition from launch.  
**Proof features:** co-op capture rituals, shared bases, raids, configurable PvP, group endgame war.  
**Fail state:** asynchronous visits only; “multiplayer coming later.”

## Pillar 5 — Nobody → Threat to Civilization
**Promise:** Memory fragments, rising notoriety, war on High Council/academies.  
**Proof features:** attention system, inquisitors, campaign ops, multi-ending aftermath world state.  
**Fail state:** pure endless sandbox with zero faction climax *or* rigid linear RPG that kills the sandbox.

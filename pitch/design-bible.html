Pitch

A discarded mage binds beasts, builds an enclave, chooses Life or Death magic, and grows until the High Council declares war.

The gap

Wizard fantasy fans got a guided open-world hit, then a vacuum: no creature economy, weak multiplayer fantasy, fenced dark magic, student-on-rails identity.

The bet

Same disruption pattern as creature-survival sandboxes vs stagnant collectors — legally original, emotionally adjacent to unfulfilled wish-fulfillment.

One-line systems

Catch with skill → put to work → automate base → path identity → Heat rises → institutions hunt you.

Core loop

Base manage→ Prepare→ Expedition→ Capture / fight→ Process→ Progress / Heat

Session target: meaningful cycle in 15–45 minutes. Always leave a clear next pin.

Each of the 12 slice beasts has a unique Soften key (pride duel, temperature, ethics fork, etc.).

Vita / Mortis

Vita — Life

  • Pacts, heal, reclaim land
  • Loyal partners, breeding quality
  • Stable compound growth
  • Lower Heat, sanctuary fantasy

Mortis — Death

  • Brands, sacrifice, corrupt
  • Thralls, mutations, spikes
  • Aggressive shortcuts
  • Higher Heat, siege fantasy

Balance rule: same investment → within ~10% clear time; different texture. Exclusive recipes are lateral.

Demo rules (non-negotiable)

Any public or investor demo must show all three by minute 15:

  1. Bond Ring capture with a readable Soften key
  2. Beast at a station producing while the player leaves
  3. Heat eye / institutional foreshadow (pamphlet or Watcher)

Seal Echo zones (anti-magic cells) force beast reliance — a differentiator from pure gun-survival fantasy.

Kill criteria

Skip capture

If pure craft-combat is more fun, capture failed.

Co-op worse

If multiplayer hurts the loop, fix net or ship solo-first.

Only adjacency

If outsiders only say “HP Palworld,” unique hooks didn’t stick.

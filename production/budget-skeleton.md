# Budget Skeleton (Indie, non-binding)

Rough order-of-magnitude for a **vertical slice** only, not 1.0.

## Team months (example)
| Role | Months | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Gameplay eng | 6–10 | Capture + AI + base |
| Online eng | 3–5 | Can be same person early |
| Design | 4–6 | Data, encounters, balance |
| Environment art | 3–5 | 2 biomes grey→pretty corner |
| Character/beast art | 4–8 | 12 beasts is heavy — grey first |
| Tech art / UI | 2–4 | Bond Ring, HUD |
| Audio | 1–2 | MVP loops + stingers |
| Producer/GA | 2–3 | Playtests, scheduling |

## Cost bands (very rough, region-dependent)
| Path | Slice ballpark |
|------|----------------|
| Ultra-lean (founder eng + contract art) | low six figures or less in cash + sweat |
| Small funded team | mid six figures |
| Polished showcase slice | upper six → low seven |

## What not to fund early
- Full VO cast  
- 100 beast final models  
- Custom engine  
- Marketing blitz before Gate A  

## Funding narrative
“We are buying a **proven loop demo** with three signature systems (capture skill, workforce, Heat) — not a vertical slice of a school tour.”
